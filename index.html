Four Story Angles
01

The Farmer Story

Farmers in Moth, Bamaur and Gursarai are drilling deeper wells each year. This report puts a 123-year record behind what they have been saying — and shows it is getting worse, not better.

02

The Heat Story

Jhansi has one of the steepest rising heat-wave trends in Uttar Pradesh. The same blocks losing rain are the ones whose heat-waves are intensifying — a one-two punch on outdoor workers and crops.

03

The Bundelkhand Question

India runs a Special Package for Bundelkhand. The data ask the obvious follow-up: in the four blocks getting drier and hotter, what is actually improving? This is investigative ground.

04

The Groundwater Story

Jhansi sits on hard-rock aquifers up to 360 metres deep, which refill slowly. With evaporation rising and rainfall splitting, the water "bank" is being drawn down faster than it is being topped up.
